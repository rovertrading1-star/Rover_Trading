Important Information

Program terms & conditions.

What happens if I miss a lesson?

You can attend the lesson when it is taught to the subsequent group. A R150 make-up lesson fee will apply.

Can I restart a chapter?

Restarting a chapter is subject to the original applicable chapter fee of R100 or R250.

Are course payments refundable?

Program policy is that educational payments are non-refundable once live classes or materials have commenced.

Will I make money after completing the program?

Trading leveraged financial instruments carries risk of loss. The training aims to instill systematic risk management and discipline.
